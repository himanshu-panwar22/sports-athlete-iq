# SIH Presentation Deck: AI-Assisted Sports Talent Discovery

---

## Slide 1: Title Slide
* **Project Name:** AI-Assisted Sports Talent Discovery Platform
* **Theme:** Sports, Fitness & Grassroots Talent Identification
* **Tagline:** Democratizing Indian sports talent discovery using standardized smartphone AI screening.
* **Core Philosophy:** *"AI assists talent discovery. AI does not make the final selection."*

---

## Slide 2: The Core Problem & Grassroots Gap
* **Geographic Exclusion:** Talent is distributed equally across India, but scouting opportunities are concentrated in metropolitan academies and state competitions.
* **Economic Barrier:** High costs of specialized testing equipment (force plates, laser timing gates) exclude government schools and rural districts.
* **Scouting Capacity Limit:** Human scouts cannot physically visit 700+ districts and 100,000+ schools.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution Funnel
```
Athlete Anywhere ──► Guided Smartphone Test ──► AI Verification & Kinematics ──► Cohort Benchmarking ──► Scout Review ──► Physical Trial
```
* **Step 1:** Athlete or teacher records standardized test on any smartphone.
* **Step 2:** Computer vision verifies attempt integrity and measures physical performance.
* **Step 3:** AI compares metrics against Indian adolescent demographic cohorts.
* **Step 4:** Human scouts inspect synchronized skeleton overlay evidence and invite high-potential athletes for physical trials.

---

## Slide 4: 4 Core Measurable MVP Assessments
1. **Countermovement Vertical Jump:** Explosive lower-body power, air flight duration ($\Delta t$), takeoff velocity ($v_0$).
2. **Bodyweight Squat & Movement Quality:** Knee angle flexion depth ($\theta$), repetition counter, bilateral left/right symmetry index.
3. **20-Meter Sprint Acceleration:** Takeoff reaction burst, cadence, split velocity across calibrated markers.
4. **Standing Broad Jump:** Horizontal explosive power and landing stability.

---

## Slide 5: Technical Innovation & Biomechanical Rigor
* **Physics Gravity Flight-Time Solver:** $h = \frac{1}{8} g (\Delta t)^2$ (independent of camera focal length).
* **One-Euro Adaptive Temporal Filter:** Eliminates landmark jitter on budget phone cameras without latency.
* **ArUco Optical Scale Calibration:** Calibrates image pixels to real-world centimeters ($S_{\text{cm/px}}$).
* **Anti-Cheat Dynamic Challenge:** Real-time randomized gesture verification in first 3 seconds.

---

## Slide 6: Ethical AI & Governance
* **DPDP Act 2023 Compliance:** Mandatory parental/guardian consent verification for athletes under 18.
* **Explainable AI (XAI):** Plain-English rationale with verified strong indicators; no black-box scores.
* **False-Negative Safe Bands:** No athlete is rejected as "having no talent"—borderline scores are routed to human review with training feedback.
* **Demographic Bias Telemetry:** Continuous auditing across Gender parity, Rural/Urban access, and phone camera quality.

---

## Slide 7: Institutional Integration Roadmap (SAI & Khelo India)
* **Stage 1 (SIH Prototype):** 4 standardized physical tests, Scout Discovery Portal, Operator Mode.
* **Stage 2 (State Federation Pilots):** Deployment in 10 Khelo India State Centres of Excellence.
* **Stage 3 (National Infrastructure):** Integration with National Sports Development Fund IDs and Digilocker.
